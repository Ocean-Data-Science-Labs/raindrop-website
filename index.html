Rain Drop is a better rain forecast. It started at a small acreage on the Mornington Peninsula and now forecasts for farms across the eastern seaboard. It's better at predicting the chance of rain, when the rain will fall and how much will fall. It also learns from your rain gauge readings, tuning the chance of rain to your farm. That means you can plan with a surer mind, all for the cost of a coffee a month.

The first month is free and then $5 a month thereafter, billed quarterly. $10 if you want us to train it on your farm gauge.

Farm Specific

We combine the world’s leading rainfall models at your farm’s exact location. Our chance of rain is about 15% more accurate than a typical single weather model.

It gets even better if you provide your rainfall data. At our trial farm, tuning to the farm’s own gauge made the chance of rain about 30% more accurate than a typical single model.

How much better will it be for me?

It's likely to improve further with readings from your own rain gauge: at our trial farm, tuning to the farm's gauge made the chance of rain about 12% more accurate again.
